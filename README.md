# Supermarket-Simulator
A 3D supermarket simulation game built with JavaScript and Three.js.
Supermarket Simulator 3D

**A 3D supermarket shopping game built with JavaScript and Three.js.**

Explore a virtual supermarket, collect items from shelves, manage your shopping budget, and complete shopping challenges before time runs out!
Supermarket Simulator is a browser-based 3D game where players navigate a virtual supermarket and shop for items from a randomly generated shopping list.
With a limited budget and a timer ticking away, every purchase matters. Find the right products, collect everything on your list, and head to the checkout before time runs out!


Features:
- 3D Environment: Explore a supermarket with aisles, shelves, and product displays.
- Interactive Shopping: Navigate the store and pick up products from shelves.
- Randomized Shopping Lists: Get a different shopping challenge each round.
- Budget Management: Start with ₹1,000 and make purchases without exceeding your available funds.
- Checkout System: Complete your shopping by checking out with your collected items.
- Timed Challenges: Race against the clock to finish your shopping.
- Level-Based Gameplay:Progress through successive shopping rounds.
- Shopping Cart: Collect products as you explore the supermarket.
- Product Catalog: Shop for groceries, dairy products, snacks, fruits, and more.


How to Play:
1. Start a shopping round and check your shopping list.
2. Explore the supermarket and locate the required products.
3. Use WASD to move.
4. Approach a product and press **E** to pick it up.
5. Keep an eye on your budget and the remaining time.
6. Collect the items on your list.
7. Head to the checkout and press **E** to complete your shopping.
8. Continue to the next level and take on a new challenge!


Objective:
Complete each shopping list within the time limit while staying within your budget.

**Can you finish your shopping before time runs out?**

Run Locally:
1. Clone this repository:

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/jPrisha07/Supermarket-Simulator
   ```

2. Open the project folder in your code editor.

3. Run the project using a local development server, such as the **Live Server** extension in VS Code.

4. Open the local URL provided by your development server in your browser.

> Note: The project uses Three.js through an ES module import, so it should be served over a local development server rather than opened directly as a file.
